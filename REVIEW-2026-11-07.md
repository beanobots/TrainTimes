## Fixes (built 2026-11-07, uncommitted)
All items are fixed. The app builds clean and server tests pass (647). Pre-fix snapshot ref: `refs/review/before-2026-11-07`. It includes untracked files, so to diff, use a temporary index with `git add -A`, then `git diff --cached <ref>`.

Changes in behaviour to check on a device:
- **Your dot on Nearby, New Trip before planning, and the landscape backdrop:** it now sits at the app's own ~100 m fix and doesn't move live as you walk. MapKit's GPS no longer runs for it.
- **Walks:**
  - GPS drops to ~100 m after 3 min without getting nearer.
  - A walk auto-ends 30 min past its expected finish with 10 min of no progress.
  - In the background, trains are fetched every 60 s when there's more than 5 min to spare, and a faster trip is looked for every 2.5 min.
- **Walking screen:**
  - The follow loop runs only while walking.
  - On the train in heading mode, the map no longer turns with the compass.
  - The compass stays on north-up while walking, for the facing cone. Turning it off there would mean dropping the cone; that's your call.
- **Turn tone:** now plays through a brief ambient audio session. Whether it sounds with the phone locked still needs a device check, because there's no `audio` background mode.
- **Route planner:**
  - Picking a station in the same complex no longer re-searches.
  - The last ticked way can't be unticked.
  - "Try Again" appears when the timetable is missing.
- **Old Citi Bike Saved Route widgets:** they show "Moved to the Citi Bike widget".
- **Server:** pushes go at priority 5 when only later trains move, and widget reloads only happen when a shown minute changes. Needs a server redeploy.
- **New untracked test file:** `server/Tests/TrainCoreTests/CountdownSignUpTests.swift`.
- **Not done:** E2's optional skipping of the fetch for a closed Citi Bike card, because the card would flash "No counts".
