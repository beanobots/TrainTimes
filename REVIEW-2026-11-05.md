## Fixed

### Bus countdown and the server (from the adversarial round)

1. **Most bus home-stop countdowns were refused by the server, so they never got pushes.**
   - The server took 1–4 lines; setup and the picker pick every line at a stop. 473 stops have more than 4 lines, and the busiest has 17.
   - A saved bus widget with a change to a train also sent its `changes`, which the server refused.
   - Every refusal was a 400, retried forever with backoff.
   - Fix: the server allows up to 20 lines and drops `changes` instead of refusing (`Registrations.swift` `busIsValid`, `HTTP.swift`). The app no longer sends `changes` either (`ActivityPush.swift`), so it also works against the server that's deployed now, except for stops with more than 4 lines.
   - New test: `registersABusAtABusyStopWithoutItsChanges`.
2. **Bus countdowns sent widget reload pushes nothing used** (`Poller.swift` `reloadWidgets`). Only train widgets read pushed countdown state, by train id, so these reloads spent the widget budget for nothing: about 10–20 per bus countdown.
3. **A local bus and its Select Bus Service at the same stop shared one key** (`Poller.swift` `busKey`). An M15 countdown could count down to M15-SBS buses. Test: `aLocalAndItsSelectBusAtOneStopAreKeptApart`.

### Server robustness

4. **A failed save made every pass resend every push** (`Registrations.swift` `apply`). A full disk or a busy lock threw before memory was updated. Each pass then repeated every priority-10 push and widget reload, and kept pushing to tokens APNs had dropped. Memory is now updated anyway, and the error is still thrown and logged.
5. **A garbled GTFS time could crash the server on every launch** (`TimetableBuilder.seconds`). A huge hour overflowed. Huge numbers are now refused; negative times still reach the trip's own checks, as the existing test expects. Test added.
6. **One slow MTA feed held up every feed's refresh** (`FeedRelay.refresh`). Fetch timeouts only count idle time. Each fetch now has an overall limit of 25 s.
7. **A feed cut off mid-download passed as good** (`SubwayFeed.isWhole`, `TrainService.isUsable`). Its header reads fine, so the relay kept the broken copy and phones failed on it. The check now also walks the top-level fields to the end. This is cheap, because it skips over them.

### App: Live Activities and walking

8. **Ending a walk left its move-on timer running** (`TrainActivityController.endWalk`). It's now cancelled, as the 11/1 fix already does for train countdowns.
9. **Two buzzes after a reroute or a switch to a faster trip** (`WalkSession`). The first was for a step you weren't at. The check now runs once, after the whole change.
10. **A failed Lock Screen countdown start, after Switch, was marked as started** (`WalkSession.switchToFaster`). The old trip's countdown then got the new trip's times under the old station. It's now retried.
11. **The compass could start while the app was in the background** (`WalkingView`). It now waits until the app is opened.
12. **After getting off underground, the walk jumped back to the boarding station** (`WalkFollower`). iOS's first location update was its old cached fix. Updates more than 10 s older than the walk's start are now ignored, as are invalid ones.
13. **A slow background refresh could lose the next refresh** (`ActivityPush.backgroundRefresh`). The next one is now scheduled first, and the server retry is skipped if iOS has cancelled the task.

### App: screens

14. **Opening the bus home stop from Spotlight** (`LiveView`). The stop was named differently from the map's own marks, so it never showed as opened. It also opened hidden under an open train page. Both fixed.
15. **Stale search results in the bus stop pickers** (`RoutePickers` `BusStopStep`, `HomeStopPicker`). When the "near you" lookup finished after a search, it replaced the search's results under "Near <search>". Old results are now dropped.
16. **First-run setup** (`FirstRun`):
    - The old address's bus stops were kept after going Back to another address.
    - Ticking a line flipped you to the other side of the street, because it reused the old list's position.
    - An old station's times could land after changing station.
    - The widgets didn't see the Citi Bike kinds chosen in setup until the app was next opened.
17. **A bus widget's walk button stayed lit up to a minute after its countdown ended** (`LeewayWidget` `routeTimeline`). It now gets an entry at the countdown's end.
18. **Bus and dock widgets had no overall time limit on their fetches** (`LeewayWidget` `routeTimeline`). On a trickling connection they could run past iOS's limit and be left blank. They now wait at most 8 s and then show "Couldn't reach Bus Time" or "Couldn't reach Citi Bike", as Nearby already does.
19. **Bus and dock widgets reloaded along with any train countdown** (`TrainWidgetIntent.trainID`). They reported the placeholder train (14 St-Union Sq), so they reloaded for its countdowns too.

### Performance

20. **`widgets.json` is decoded once per change, not 5+ times per widget reload** (`SharedStore.loadWidgets`). It's cached by modification date, as settings already are.
21. **The bus routes file reads faster, and the line list is sorted once** (`BusRoutes`). It's also decoded off the main thread when the home stop picker opens (`HomeStopPicker`).
22. **The nearest stop on a bus line's stop list is worked out once per draw, not once per row** (`HomeStopPicker`). It was O(n²).
23. **The dock picker sort works out each distance once** (`RoutePickers` `DockStep`).
24. **The New Trip station search runs once per draw** (`NewTripView` `TripPlaceList`).
25. **No Apple Maps walk is requested for own-bike ends 25 m or less apart** (`TripMap.streetWalk`). This saves directions quota.
26. **Walk routing finds the start and end sidewalks once, not up to 5 times** (`WalkRouter.route`).
27. **The sidewalk inside-test casts its ray toward the nearer edge** (`Sidewalks.inside`).
28. **`Change.isAhead` uses precomputed branch positions** (`Stations.branchPositions`). It's called thousands of times per plan.

## Not fixed: your call

### Medium

- **A. Bus Time quota abuse** (`Poller.swift`). A countdown with a made-up stop is asked about every 20 s and never pushed. 5 keys could use up the 100,000-a-day allowance for everyone. Options:
  - (a) wait at least 60 s between asks for a never-pushed countdown
  - (b) keep a stop's failed answer for a few minutes
  - (c) count asks against the owner's limit

  I'd do (a) and (b).
- **B. Every-line Bus Time asks return 6 visits in total, not per line** (`BusTime.swift:380`). At busy stops your line can drop out, and the server pushes "no next buses" or nothing. Fix: add `MinimumStopVisitsPerLine=3` to every-line asks, which also changes Nearby's answers. Check against the MTA SIRI docs first.
- **C. Bus countdowns cost about 2 priority-10 pushes per bus.** There's no `later` refill after a move-on. Train countdowns also send a second push and widget reload after each move-on, because `trip` goes "" and is then refilled (`ActivityState.isMeaningful`/`changesWidgets`). I'd fix both. They're small, but they change push logic, so I left them.
- **D. A Current Location fix of any age is used for Go and for setting Home/Work** (`NewTripView`). Leave the tab for hours, come back, tap Go: the walk starts from the old fix. Fix: re-locate when the app becomes active, and only use a fix less than 2 minutes old.
- **E. Approximate Location isn't handled.** With Precise off, a walk never arrives or reroutes, and nothing says why. Fix: ask for temporary full accuracy for walks (an `Info.plist` purpose key), and show a "Precise Location is off" note.
- **F. A walk you never mark as at the station or on the train tracks for 2 hours** (`WalkSession`). It stays at best accuracy and can keep rerouting. Option: board automatically when you're more than 300 m from the station and moving faster than 8 m/s.
- **G. The Nearby widget does a full fetch every 15 min while showing only its static card.** Option: every 60 min with fewer entries while it's closed.
- **H. A bus Saved Route on the Lock Screen asks to reload every 5 min**, far past the budget, and its entries end after 20 min. Options: entries up to the last known bus (about 60 min), and 10–15 min reloads on the Lock Screen.
- **I. An empty but valid train feed pushes "No trains predicted"** (`FeedRelay`). For example, during an MTA restart. Option: keep the old copy until 3 empty reads in a row. That's a tradeoff, since a suspended line has to go empty eventually.

### Low

- **J. Lock Screen bus and train faces show a frozen "0:00"** where Home Screen boards say "At Stop" or "At Station". Fix: add an `atLabel` to `LockFace`. It follows the At Station design, but it's a visible change.
- **K. Live Activity "then 9 minutes" may count up once that train has gone** (`TrainLiveActivity.swift:513`). Check on a device.
- **L. One failed bus widget reload throws away buses that are still good** (`RouteBoards` `SavedBusBoard.fetch`). Trains keep their last board. Option: save the last good bus board.
- **M. Nearby offline: buses stay green, then read "No buses due."** Option: a "Bus times couldn't be updated" state.
- **N. The app and the server can count down to different buses** when a home stop's line only runs on the other side of the street (`BusTrips.swift:310`). Option: keep only arrivals at the chosen stop.
- **O. A Spotlight home stop opened far away** reframes back to you, and on a cold launch doesn't move the map at all (`LiveView`). Option: set `mapCenter`. Check on a device.
- **P. Coming back to Nearby loads twice** (resume, then the fresh fix's `loadKey`).
- **Q. A failed timetable download is retried on every widget reload and app open** (`TimetableStore`). Option: wait about an hour after a failure.
- **R. Unauthenticated `/feeds` with random `stops` forces a full cut and gzip per request** (`FeedRelay`). Option: cap concurrent cuts at about 4.
- **S. A Bus Time hang can delay the poll pass's train countdowns** (`Poller.swift:187`). Option: fetch buses alongside the trains.
- **T. Offline messages that are wrong:**

  - `NewTripView`/`TripPlaceList` `@State` starting values are re-created on every rebuild.
  - `WalkRouter.blocks(along:)` does about 1–2M distance checks per reroute.
  - `TrainService.isUsable` fully parses the 1 MB alerts JSON just to check it.
- **V. Smaller ones:**
  - A 401 deletes a diagnostics report.
